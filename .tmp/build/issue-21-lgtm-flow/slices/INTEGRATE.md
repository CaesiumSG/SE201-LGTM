* CT-reviewer-reach-1 and CT-benchmark-scope-trace-1 drift: confirm the keys persisted and consumed match.
* README.md and benchmark/README.md are edited by two slices; keep both sides.
* Model endpoint: only INTEGRATE runs live models, so no lease contention.
* DEC-9: no real-world result may feed back into prompts, budgets, or judge criteria. Run the real-world set once, after the final Shoply-based change; a failure is reported, not tuned.
